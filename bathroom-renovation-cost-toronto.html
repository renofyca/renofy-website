Bathroom renovation cost in Toronto.

Real numbers for the GTA — what a bathroom actually costs in 2026, where the money goes, and how to get the spa feel without the spa bill. From the crew that builds them every week.

The short answer

In Toronto and the GTA in 2026, a full bathroom renovation typically costs $28,000–$60,000. A powder room refresh runs $15,000–$25,000, and a luxury primary ensuite with curbless shower and heated floors reaches $60,000–$90,000.

What drives the cost up or down

  • Moving plumbing. The single biggest cost adder. Relocating a toilet or shower drain means opening floors and re-routing stacks — keeping fixtures roughly where they are saves thousands.
  • Shower complexity. A curbless walk-in shower with a linear drain, bench, and niche costs notably more than a standard alcove tub-shower — and it's the feature clients love most. Worth it if the budget allows.
  • Waterproofing. The invisible work that matters most. Proper membrane waterproofing behind every wet wall is non-negotiable — this is where cheap contractors cut corners and where leaks are born.
  • Tile & stone. Large-format porcelain keeps grout lines minimal; natural stone and quartz vanity tops push the budget up. (Note: we don't do tile backsplashes in kitchens, but bathroom tile work is a different craft — ours is precise enough to silence a contractor brother-in-law.)
  • Fixtures & finishes. A wall-hung toilet, matte-black fixtures, and a heated floor add comfort and cost. Pick your splurges: heated floors are the one clients say they'd never give up.
  • Condo vs. house. Condo bathrooms add logistics — elevator bookings, restricted work hours, board approvals. Budget a little extra time and patience.

What's typically included

A proper quote covers: demolition and disposal, plumbing rough-in and fixtures, electrical (including heated floors and ventilation), waterproofing membrane, tile installation, vanity and stone top, shower glass, painting, and final cleanup. Ventilation deserves a special mention — an undersized fan in a Toronto winter means moisture problems. We size it right.

The spa feel, without the spa bill

  • Lighting layers. A dimmable overhead plus warm sconce lighting transforms a bathroom for a few hundred dollars.
  • One statement surface. A quartz vanity top or a feature wall in natural stone gives the luxury read; keep the rest simple.
  • Heated floors. The highest happiness-per-dollar upgrade in any bathroom renovation. Clients mention it more than any other feature.
  • Keep the layout. Same advice as kitchens — the existing drain locations are money in your pocket.

How long does it take?

Most GTA bathroom renovations take 3–5 weeks. The critical path is tile and waterproofing cure times — anyone promising a full bathroom in a week is skipping steps you'll pay for later.
