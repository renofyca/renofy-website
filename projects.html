From the job site

Three projects, three happy streets.

Etobicoke · Kitchen renovation

The chef's kitchen that started with a wall

A 1970s Etobicoke bungalow with a closed-off galley kitchen. We removed the wall to the dining room (engineered beam, permits handled), installed custom cabinetry to the ceiling, and finished it with a full-height quartzite backsplash and waterfall island. Six weeks, fixed quote, finished four days early — the clients now host every family dinner. See what a kitchen like this costs in Toronto.

Vaughan · Bathroom renovation

From builder-basic to boutique hotel

This Vaughan primary ensuite had the standard builder package: one-piece tub, tiny shower, beige everything. We went curbless with a linear drain, added a bench and niche, heated floors throughout, and layered the lighting. Four weeks, and the clients say mornings feel like a hotel stay. Details on bathroom renovation costs in the GTA.

Mississauga · Basement finishing

Dead square footage becomes the best room in the house

An unfinished Mississauga basement — concrete, joists, and potential. We added a media lounge with acoustic treatment, a guest suite with a full bathroom, and a home gym corner, all with proper egress windows and permits. The family's living space effectively doubled. Every basement we finish carries the same 1-year craftsmanship warranty as our kitchens.
