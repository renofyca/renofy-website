Our story

Built in the GTA, built different.

Renofy started the way the best companies do — with frustration. After watching friends get burned by vanishing contractors and budgets that doubled mid-project, we built the renovation company we wished existed: licensed, insured, and allergic to excuses. Twelve years and 250+ projects later, we're the crew Toronto recommends at dinner parties.

The fixed-price thing isn't marketing; it's the whole business model. We scope obsessively, price honestly, and put it in writing before day one. If we misjudge, that's on us — your quote doesn't move unless you change the scope. Clients tell us it's the first time a contractor's number actually meant something.

And then there's the dust. Ask any Renofy client about the negative-air scrubbers, the sealed zip-walls, the end-of-day sweep that happens whether you're watching or not. Renovations are messy by nature — living through one shouldn't be. We obsess over the details you'll never see so you only notice the ones you will.
